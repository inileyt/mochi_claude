Mochi on every site

Optional. Only for the things below, and you can turn it off here.

What it's for

Live Edits you keep: your changes are applied every time you open that site, even before Mochi comes.

Guides: Mochi comes along to the next page of a guide by himself, so you don't have to click him again.

Note reminders use the same permission (Settings → Note reminders).

Chrome will ask to let Mochi "read and change your data on all websites". That's Chrome's general wording: Mochi only adds your kept CSS and comes along on guides you started. It reads nothing else and sends nothing anywhere.
