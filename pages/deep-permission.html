Allow Deep Scan

To see hover states, other screen widths, animations and full-page pictures, Mochi needs Chrome's "debugger" access for the tab you scan.

  • Only the tab you scan, only while the scan runs, then it's closed again.
  • Nothing leaves your browser. No passwords, cookies or network traffic are read.
  • Mochi never submits forms, types, follows links or presses buy / delete / send buttons.

While a scan runs, Chrome shows a bar: "Mochi started debugging this browser". That's normal; it goes away when the scan ends. Pressing Cancel on it stops the scan.
