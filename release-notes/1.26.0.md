### What's new in 1.26.0

- 💬 **ChatGPT and Gemini (optional):** Mochi can now keep you company there too. Turn them on in Mochi → Settings → *Chat sites*; Chrome asks you first, and turning a site off gives the permission back. claude.ai works exactly as before.
- 🔄 **Settings that follow you:** with Chrome sync on, Mochi's settings, color, name, level, XP, streak and unlocked items travel to your other computers (Chrome's own sync; Mochi still makes no network requests).
- 👋 **A proper hello:** a welcome page after installing (pick Mochi's color and a name, choose sites), a three-card tour the first time in a chat (Settings → *Replay the tour*), and a one-time "What's new" bubble after updates.
- 🎂 **Mochi's birthday:** little parties at 7 days, 1 month and 100 days, and every year on the install day a party hat, a cake with one candle per year to blow out, a birthday hat to keep, and a share card.
- 🛟 If a chat site changes its page, Mochi stays as a plain companion instead of guessing.

### What Mochi can do (1.26.0)

- 🟧 **A living pixel companion** next to claude.ai (and ChatGPT / Gemini if you enable them) that reacts to your chat, with moods, idle antics, a wardrobe, 7 color themes and friends (cat, robot, ghost)
- ⭐ **Levels, XP and a daily streak**, unlocking outfits, friends and tricks; a share card
- 🎂 **Birthdays and milestones**, with a cake, candles and a birthday hat
- 🌐 **Online friends (optional)**: Mochi codes, requests, chat and visits; off until you turn it on
- 🎯 **Pick an element** and 🔎 **Scan this UI** of any site into an AI prompt, DESIGN.md, CSS, Tailwind or JSON; Mochi's pocket brings styles back to Claude
- 💡 Prompt tips, a prompt library, answer bookmarks, chat export to Markdown, a long-chat warning and a "Claude answered" tab badge
- ⏱ Focus mode (Pomodoro), a mini-game while the AI thinks, 8-bit sounds, explore mode, an outfit editor, and a few secrets 🤫
- 🗣 **11 languages**: English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Հայերեն, Italiano, 日本語, Português, Русский, Українська, 简体中文
