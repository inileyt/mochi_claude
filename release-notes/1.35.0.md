### What's new in 1.35.0

- 🌍 **Image Scan reads more languages — your pick.** Besides English, Russian and Armenian, you can now turn on **German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Ukrainian and Greek**. Choose the ones you read on the **welcome page** or in **Settings → Image text languages**. Only the languages you turn on are loaded, so scans stay fast — and every model is bundled, so it all still works fully offline.
