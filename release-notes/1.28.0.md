### What's new in 1.28.0

- 🔍 **Image scan:** right-click any picture → **Scan image with Mochi** (or the ⌕ magnifier on big pictures, or Menu → Tools → Scan an image). Mochi shows its **palette** (exact hex, RGB, HSL, share, names like "dusty rose", roles, light/dark, warm/cool, gradients; copy as CSS variables or Tailwind), the **text** in it (English and Russian OCR), the **font style** (category, weight, width, case, spacing, and the 3 closest of 20 free fonts — approximate, and it says so), **what it shows** (Chrome's on-device AI when available), and a ready **AI image prompt** for Midjourney, Stable Diffusion/SDXL and DALL·E/GPT-image/Imagen, plus alt text.
- ✎ **Ask Claude about this image:** copies the picture and a ready question, opens claude.ai, and pastes both into the message box. You press Enter yourself.
- 🖼 Works with pictures from other sites, SVG, canvas, CSS backgrounds and pictures taller than the window. Mochi hides while the picture is taken; **Cancel** stops a scan; the page never freezes.
- 🔐 New permissions without warnings: `contextMenus` (the right-click item) and `offscreen` (a hidden worker page). Everything runs on your computer: the OCR engine, its English/Russian files and the fonts are bundled (the extension is now about 6.5 MB).
- Fixes: a newer item in the pocket (like an image to ask Claude about) replaces an older offer still on screen; the panel no longer reappears when closed right after opening.
