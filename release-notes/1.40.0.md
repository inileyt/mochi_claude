### What's new in 1.40.0

Two new scanners under **Menu → Tools → Scanners**:

- 🎞 **Scan a video.** Pick a `<video>` and Mochi reads it without touching it: its real and displayed size, duration, **sources** (and their types), **poster**, and the attributes that decide how it plays (autoplay, loop, muted, controls, object-fit…). It grabs a still frame when the browser allows it — a cross-origin video's pixels can't be read, so the poster is used instead — writes clean `<video>` HTML to recreate it, and a prompt for Claude.
- ✨ **Scan the hero.** Mochi finds the big section at the top of the page (or the element you pick) and captures the lovely bits that make it move: **CSS `@keyframes` animations** are kept exactly; **scroll / entrance reveals** (elements that fade or slide in) are rebuilt as a reusable reveal animation plus a tiny `IntersectionObserver` snippet (approximate, since those are usually driven by JavaScript); and a **`<canvas>` drawn in code** is detected — including whether it's a **3D / WebGL scene** (Three.js and friends), with a still frame and a prompt to recreate it on a canvas. For reveals you can also press **⟲ Record reveals**: Mochi scrolls each below-the-fold element into view and **samples its real motion**, rebuilding the exact reveal keyframes instead of an estimate. You get the CSS to reuse and a prompt for Claude. Nothing is downloaded; the page isn't changed.
