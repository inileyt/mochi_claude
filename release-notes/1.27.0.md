### What's new in 1.27.0

- 🔬 **Deep scan (rebuild spec):** Mochi studies the page you're on and writes a zip for an AI coding agent — `PROMPT.md`, `SPEC.md`, `spec.json`, `tokens.css`, `tailwind.config.js` and screenshots. It captures the structure, exact sizes and layouts, the page at 1440/1024/768/390 px, design tokens, components with hover/focus/click states, animations (CSS and JavaScript), and a scroll timeline (sticky headers, reveals, parallax).
- 🔐 Uses Chrome's **optional `debugger` permission**, asked once after Mochi explains it; only for the scanned tab, only during the scan (Chrome shows a "started debugging" bar), always closed afterwards.
- 🛡 **Never acts for you:** no forms, no typing, no links, no buy/delete/send/sign-up buttons (English and Russian), navigation blocked during the scan, everything put back.
- 🎭 **Placeholders by default:** logos, brand names, pictures and texts become placeholders of the same size and shape (screenshots too); tick "this is my own site" to keep them.
- Nothing leaves your browser. ChatGPT/Gemini: Mochi now notices answers even when the Stop button looks different.
