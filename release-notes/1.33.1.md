### What's new in 1.33.1

Two small quality-of-life fixes for the companion:

- 🧲 **Bringing Mochi back is obvious now.** When you tuck Mochi away (the **–** button), he rests as a peeking sliver at the edge of the window. He now shows a little **‹ pull-handle** and says "Bring Mochi back", so it's clear you can click him to return — before, the way back was easy to miss.
- ⌖ **"Pick another" is right where you need it.** After *Pick an element*, the pick-again button used to sit at the very bottom of the result, below the export box. It now lives in the always-visible footer, so you can pick the next element without scrolling or reopening the menu.
