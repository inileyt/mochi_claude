### What's new in 1.33.1

Small quality-of-life fixes for the companion:

- ‹ **A back arrow on every result.** Each result panel (Pick an element, Scan this UI, a picked color, and so on) now has a **‹ back** button that returns to Mochi's menu — so you can switch to another tool without closing Mochi and reopening it.
- ⌖ **"Pick another" where you need it.** After *Pick an element*, the pick-again button used to sit at the very bottom of the result, below the export box. It now lives in the always-visible footer, so you can pick the next element without scrolling.
- 🧲 **Bringing Mochi back is obvious now.** When you tuck Mochi away (the **–** button), he rests as a peeking sliver at the edge of the window. He now shows a little **‹ pull-handle** and says "Bring Mochi back", so it's clear you can click him to return.
