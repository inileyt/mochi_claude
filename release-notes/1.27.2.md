### What's new in 1.27.2

- 🐱 **Your character stays on every page.** After a level-up unlocked the cat (or the robot, the ghost) and you picked it, a newly opened page could show plain Mochi while the wardrobe still said the cat was chosen. Mochi now puts the chosen character back on as soon as your level is loaded, and when your level changes on another tab or computer.
- Update: replace the files in your `mochi-extension` folder, press ↻ on Mochi's card at `chrome://extensions`, reload the page.
