### What's new in 1.27.1

- 🔬 **Deep scan works now.** Chrome doesn't allow the `debugger` permission to be optional, so asking for it later always failed ("Okay, not allowed"). It is now part of Mochi's permissions and does nothing until you press **Start deep scan**; only that tab, only during the scan.
- When Deep scan can't start, Mochi explains why (DevTools or another debugging extension on the tab, a protected Chrome page, a missing permission) with **Try again**.
- Update: replace the files in your `mochi-extension` folder, press ↻ on Mochi's card at `chrome://extensions`, reload the page.
