### What's new in 1.30.0

- ✨ **Live Edit:** pick any part of any page (or the whole page), say what should change, and Mochi takes it to Claude. When Claude answers, Mochi carries the CSS back and applies it: before/after, edit, tweak, undo, or keep it for the site. Only CSS, checked first: nothing that runs code or loads from the web.
- 🧭 **Mochi Guides:** an answer with steps becomes a guide on the real website. Mochi finds each button, walks over and points at it, and moves on when you've done it. Record your own guides and share them as files. Mochi never clicks for you.
- 🎨 **Your design taste:** Mochi learns the colors, fonts and vibe you keep coming back to (in your browser only) and gives Claude a design brief in one click.
- Optional "Mochi on every site" (asked from a click, explained first): kept Live Edits on every visit, guides that follow you to the next page.
