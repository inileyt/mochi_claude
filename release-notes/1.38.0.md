### What's new in 1.38.0

- 🗂️ **Tools menu, drill-down.** The Tools tab is now a short list of **categories** — *Scanners*, *Inspect & grab*, *With Claude*, *Notes on this page* and *In this chat* — and you open one to see just its tools, with a *‹* to go back. The list stays short and easy to scan as more tools are added. (The other four tabs are unchanged.)
- 🔗 **New scanner — Broken links & images.** Menu → Tools → *Scanners* → **Broken links & images** gives any page a quick health check, read **locally — nothing is fetched, nothing leaves your browser**: images that failed to load, images missing `alt` text (an accessibility problem), and dead links (empty / `#` / `javascript:` links, and in-page `#id` links whose target isn't on the page). Click a result and Mochi scrolls to it and outlines it; copy a report, or hand it to Claude to fix. (Links to other sites aren't fetched, so their 404s can't be seen here.)
