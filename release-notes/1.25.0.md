### What Mochi can do (1.25.0)

- 🟧 **A living pixel companion** next to claude.ai that reacts to your chat, with moods, idle antics, a wardrobe, 7 color themes and friends (cat, robot, ghost)
- ⭐ **Levels, XP and a daily streak**, unlocking outfits, friends and tricks; a share card
- 🌐 **Online friends (new, optional)**: give a friend your Mochi code, accept requests, chat, and your Mochis visit each other's screens; a shared friendship level unlocks a matching scarf. Off until you turn it on
- 🎯 **Pick an element**: now also animations (with @keyframes), SVG illustrations, images, canvas, video, background images and web components; Space steps through stacked layers
- 🔎 **Scan this UI** of any site into an AI prompt, DESIGN.md, CSS, Tailwind or JSON; Mochi's pocket brings styles back to Claude
- 💡 Prompt tips, a prompt library, answer bookmarks, chat export to Markdown, a long-chat warning and a "Claude answered" tab badge
- ⏱ Focus mode (Pomodoro), a mini-game while Claude thinks, 8-bit sounds, explore mode, an outfit editor, and a few secrets 🤫
- 🗣 **11 languages**: English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Հայերեն, Italiano, 日本語, Português, Русский, Українська, 简体中文
