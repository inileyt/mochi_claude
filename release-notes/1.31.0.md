### What's new in 1.31.0

- 🐞 **Bug Reporter:** press Record, do what goes wrong, press Stop. Mochi has written the steps (never what you typed), caught the page's errors and failed requests, and taken a screenshot after each step. Get the report for Claude (to find the cause), as a GitHub issue, or as a zip. Nothing leaves your computer until you copy it.
- ◇ **Copy for Figma:** pick any part of a page and paste it into Figma as editable layers (shapes, text, pictures, icons), or download it as .svg.
- The DevTools connection (the `debugger` permission, already there for Deep scan) now has a lighter report mode for the Bug Reporter: it only listens, only in that tab, and closes when you stop.
