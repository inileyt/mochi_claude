### What's new in 1.41.0

- 🧹 **A tidier Tools menu.** With all the new scanners the menu had grown long, so a few things merged. **Deep scan** is now the single way to scan a whole page (the old "Scan this UI" is gone — Deep scan covers it). **Scan animation** folded into **Scan the hero**: pick a single small element there and Mochi captures just its animation, with the live preview; pick a big section and you get the hero scan. And the **In this chat** group dropped its three duplicate shortcuts (Bookmark this answer, Export chat, Replay last guide — the full Bookmarks, Export and Guides stay). Scanners went from seven items to five.
- ⟲ **Record reveals (Scan the hero).** The reveal CSS used to be an estimate. Now press **Record reveals** and Mochi scrolls each below-the-fold element into view, samples its real transform/opacity frame by frame while the page's own transition plays, and rebuilds the exact reveal `@keyframes`. The page is restored afterwards; 3D/WebGL heroes skip it.
